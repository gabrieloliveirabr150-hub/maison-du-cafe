Our Story

Coffee, Considered

Why we built a room around something as small as a cup of coffee.

Maison du Café began with a simple frustration: good coffee shouldn't require a compromise between quality and atmosphere. So we built both — a bar serious enough for people who care about extraction time, and a room comfortable enough to spend an afternoon in.

Our Philosophy

Quality is a decision made in advance.

We taste every batch before it goes on the bar. Grinders are dialled in by hand, shot by shot, until the balance is right — not once a week, but as the day's temperature and humidity shift.

It means slower mornings and the occasional wait. We think the cup is worth it, and so do the people who keep coming back.

"We would rather make forty excellent cups a day than four hundred forgettable ones."

The Room

A space designed to slow you down.

Dark wood, low light and worn textures — the room was built to feel lived-in from the first week, not polished for a photograph. We wanted somewhere people would choose to stay, not just pass through.

Tables are spaced for conversation or solitude, whichever the afternoon calls for.

What We Hold To

Three things that don't change

Sourcing

We choose beans for character and traceability, and rotate our single origins through the year.

Craft

Every method on the menu — espresso, pour-over, cold brew — is brewed to a standard, not a shortcut.

Hospitality

We remember names, we remember orders, and we'd rather talk you through the menu than rush you.

Thoughtful Preparation

Brewed to the method, not around it.

Pour-over asks for patience; espresso asks for precision. We treat each brewing method on its own terms instead of forcing one approach onto every cup.

Ask your barista which method suits the bean you're curious about — they'll have an opinion.
