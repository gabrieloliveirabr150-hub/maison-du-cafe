Specialty, Not Incidental

Coffee treated like a craft, not a commodity.

Every cup starts with beans chosen for character, roasted in small batches and dialled in by hand, one grinder setting at a time. We brew to taste, not to the clock.

It is a slower way to run a coffee bar. We think it is the only way worth running one.
